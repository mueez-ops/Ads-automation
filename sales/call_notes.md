# Sales call notes

Log each call below with a dated heading (`## YYYY-MM-DD — Brand — Contact`).

---

## Discovery call agenda (20 minutes)

1. **Introductions (2 min):** who Halfsecond is and how the call will run.
2. **Their business (8 min):** the qualification questions below.
3. **What I'd do (5 min):** share 2–3 findings from the teardown and what the pilot would test.
4. **Fit and next step (5 min):** if it fits, agree to a proposal and a decision date. If not, say so honestly.

## Qualification questions

- [ ] Current monthly revenue (rough range is fine)
- [ ] Current monthly ad budget, and on which platforms
- [ ] Ads run before? What happened, and what did they learn?
- [ ] Average order value and gross margin. Use these to work out break-even ROAS (1 ÷ gross margin).
- [ ] Fulfilment and customer-service capacity if orders double
- [ ] Main goal: more purchases / lower CPA / higher AOV / new market
- [ ] Who approves a pilot, and by when?
- [ ] Access to Meta Business Manager, pixel/CAPI and Shopify analytics

**Disqualify if:** they want guaranteed sales, have no product traction, have an unrealistic budget (under about £1,000/month in ad spend for a test), or show poor fulfilment signals.

## Honest answers to common questions

Never invent results, clients or testimonials. Never misstate where you're based. Here's what to say instead.

**"Do you have case studies or results?"**
> "I'm early in building Halfsecond, so I don't have case studies to show you yet, and I won't pretend otherwise. That's why I start with a free teardown and a short pilot: you see how I think before you pay, you get weekly reports, and you decide after 30 days. I'm also taking on my first few brands at a founding rate."

**"Who else have you worked with?"**
> "No retained clients yet. You'd be one of my first, which means you get my full attention and a reduced rate. In return I'd ask for an honest testimonial if the results earn it."

**"Where are you based?" / time zone**
> "I work remotely and keep UK business hours, so you'll get same-day replies and calls in your working day."
> (This is true. If they ask for your exact location, give the real one. Don't name a different country.)

**"Can you guarantee results?"**
> "No one honestly can. What I can promise is a structured test, clear reporting, and a straight recommendation at the end, including telling you if paid ads aren't the right lever yet."

**"It's too expensive."**
> "Fair. The pilot is scoped so the learnings are useful even if we stop after 30 days. We could also cut the creative volume to bring the fee down."
