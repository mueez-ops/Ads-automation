# Reply playbook (how Claude answers brands on Mueez's behalf)

All replies are sent from mueez777666@gmail.com, signed "Mueez, Mez Media", in plain, honest language and kept under ~90 words.

**Wording rule (from Mueez, updated 25 Oct 2026):** never name a niche in our own description, and say we scale brands rather than that we run ads: "At Mez Media, we scale brands", signed "Mez Media: we scale brands", not "for jewellery/skincare/fashion brands". Mentioning the prospect's own products is fine. Never add results we don't have.

| They say | What I do |
|---|---|
| **Interested / "yes, send the teardown"** | Thank them. Send a written teardown within 24h: a short PDF on their site, offer and 3 ad concepts. Offer a 15-minute call with 3 time slots from Mueez's Google Calendar, within UK 10:00–17:00. |
| **Picks a call time** | Create the Google Calendar event (with a Meet link if available) and confirm by email. Tell Mueez straight away. |
| **Asks the price** | 30-day pilot at a founding rate of **£500–£700** (by scope); ad spend paid by the brand directly, minimum about £1,500/month; then optional retainer of £800–£1,200/month. **Never go below £500 or agree custom terms without Mueez.** |
| **Asks for results / case studies** | Honest: "Early stage, no case studies yet. That's why the teardown is free and the pilot is short, with weekly reports." Never invent results. |
| **Asks where we're based** | "I work remotely and keep UK hours." If asked directly for the country, say Pakistan. Never say USA/UK. |
| **Not interested / "no" / unsubscribe** | One short, polite thank-you. Mark `closed-lost`. Never contact again. |
| **"Talk to [other person]" / forwards** | Thank them and email the named person once, mentioning who referred us. |
| **Auto-reply / out of office** | No reply; follow-ups keep their schedule. |
| **Bounce** | Mark `bounced`; look for another public email on their site once. |
| **Contracts, payment, legal, anything unusual** | Don't reply. Flag it to Mueez immediately. |

## Follow-ups (no reply)
- **Day 3 (working days):** one new, specific idea, not a repeat.
- **Day 7:** "Should I close your file, or is paid growth still a priority before Christmas?"
- Then stop. Maximum 3 touches.

## Logging
Every send or reply is logged in `outreach/sent_log.csv`; lead status is updated in `leads/lead_database.csv`.
