## Post 2: Carousel "Break-even ROAS in 10 seconds" · Mon 19 Oct

Make it in Canva, 6 slides, big text, plain background.

1. **Your ROAS target is probably wrong.**
2. Break-even ROAS = **1 ÷ your profit margin**
3. Example: you sell a facial oil for **£30**. Making + shipping + fees cost **£9**. Margin = 70%.
4. 1 ÷ 0.70 = **1.43**. Below 1.43, every new order loses money.
5. Want to beat it? Sell a **set** (e.g. £55) instead of one item. The ad costs the same, but the basket is bigger.
6. **Comment "ROAS" and I'll work out yours.** (Halfsecond logo)

**Caption:** `Most skincare brands set their ROAS target by gut feel. Work out your break-even first. Comment ROAS and I'll calculate yours.`

## Post 3: Short video "3 things I check in a skincare ad" · Tue 20 Oct (morning, before the emails go out)

30–40 seconds. Use a voiceover on a screen recording of any public skincare ad from Meta Ad Library.

1. **First 2 seconds:** is there a reason to stop scrolling? (movement, texture, a face, a bold line of text)
2. **The offer:** is one cheap product carrying the whole ad cost? A set often works better.
3. **The click:** does the website page show the same product the ad promised?
