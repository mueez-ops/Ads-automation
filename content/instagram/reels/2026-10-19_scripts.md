# Reel scripts: Mon 19 Oct 2026 (3 reels)

Faceless, black and white motion graphics. Mueez records the voiceover; Claude builds the video to his timing. Every line was timed with beats.py (170 wpm) and checked with the humanizer.

## How to record (all 3 in one go, about 5 minutes)
- Quiet room, soft furnishings (a bedroom is better than a kitchen). Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- iPhone: **Voice Memos**. Android: **Recorder**. One file per reel.
- Read each line, pause 1 second, next line. Fluffed a line? Pause and say it again; I cut the mistakes.
- Calm and confident, a bit faster than normal talking. Smile slightly on the hook; it changes your tone.
- Send the 3 files here, named reel1, reel2, reel3.
