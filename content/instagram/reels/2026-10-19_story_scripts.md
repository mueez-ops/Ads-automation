# Story reel scripts: 19 Oct 2026 (replaces the tip-list scripts)

Format: true, public ad stories told like the Breaking News reel. Super hook in frame 1 (image + big text up before the first word), story with a twist, the lesson, the DM ask, and a last line that echoes the hook. Every number is from published reporting; 'reported' is said out loud where the figure isn't official.
