## Reel 1: 3 a.m. (Maya, the candle maker)

**Visual plan:** Frame 1: dark bedroom, phone screen lighting a face, buzz SFX, clock 03:00. Notification card '40,000 people reached / 0 sales'. Kitchen candle photos, the brother's thumb flicking past in 0.5s (freeze + '0.5s' stamp), match strike + flame in slow motion, final notification 'New order' with a soft chime.

```
3 a.m. Maya's phone buzzes.
It's not an order.
"Your ad reached 40,000 people."
Zero sales.
Maya makes candles in her tiny kitchen.
She's spent 2,000 pounds on ads this month.
Her savings.
Next morning, her little brother is scrolling.
Her ad comes up.
He skips it in half a second.
"Why did you skip it?"
He shrugs. "It's just your logo, Maya."
She stares at the screen.
Every ad she'd made started the same way. Her logo.
So she films something new.
A match. A flame. Wax melting in slow motion.
Logo? Last second.
Three nights later, 3 a.m.
Her phone buzzes again.
This time, it's an order.
Views, but no orders?
DM me "teardown" before your phone buzzes again.
```

Daniel side: red 'SALE 20% OFF' boosts repeating; Omar side: B&W rain, girl laughing in a leather jacket. Box opening, close-ups of identical stitching, 'SAME FACTORY' stamp. End: two shops, one light goes out.

```
Two shops. Same city. Same jackets.
One closed in six months.
Daniel and Omar both sell leather jackets online.
Daniel's ads?
"20% off. Shop now."
Every week, the same boost.
Omar's ads?
A girl in the rain.
Her jacket's soaked. She laughs. She doesn't care.
Omar says nothing about price.
Daniel watches Omar's sales climb.
He buys an Omar jacket to see what's so special.
He opens the box.
Same stitching. Same leather. Same factory.
Same product.
Daniel sold a discount.
Omar sold a feeling.
Which one are your ads selling?
DM me "teardown".
Two shops. Only one survives.
```

## Reel 3: The intern (Sophie vs the agency)

**Visual plan:** Frame 1: two phone screens side by side, a glossy ad vs a shaky selfie, 'FREE' vs '10K' price tags, the free one winning a tug-of-war bar. Glossy studio photos (cold), a phone on a shelf (warm, real), James checking numbers at 7 a.m., comments flooding up the screen.

```
The intern's free video beat a 10k ad.
Here's how.
Sophie is 19. First week at a skincare brand.
The founder, James, has just paid an agency 10,000 pounds.
Perfect models. Perfect lighting. Perfect skin.
The ad goes live.
Nobody stops.
On Friday, Sophie asks, "Can I try something?"
She props her phone on a shelf.
No makeup. Her real skin. Her real breakouts.
"This is me before. Watch me for 30 days."
James almost says no.
Monday morning, he checks the numbers.
Her video is beating the agency ad.
Comments flooding in. "Finally, someone real."
James asks, "What did you change?"
Sophie smiles. "I stopped pretending."
People don't stop for perfect.
They stop for real.
Want ads like the intern's? DM me "teardown".
```
