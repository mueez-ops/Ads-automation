# Niche decision (17 Oct 2026)

**Decision: UK independent jewellery as the main niche for Q4, with skincare kept as the second lane.** Clothing and "all e-commerce" are on hold.

| Niche | Margin | Ad fit | Competition from agencies | Timing now | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jewellery (demi-fine, handmade)** | High (often 60–80%) | Very visual, gift-driven, small items film well | Lots of small founder-led brands with no agency | **Christmas gifting starts now** | **Main niche** |
| Skincare | High | Good, but UK ad rules (ASA) limit claims | Medium | Steady | Keep: 9 emails already prepared |
| Clothing | Lower; returns 20–40% | Good | Crowded with agencies and freelancers | Peak, but crowded | Not now |
| All e-commerce | Mixed | Mixed | — | — | Too broad: messages can't be specific |

## Who we target
- UK founder-led brands, roughly £10k–£200k/month: Shopify site, ad pixel installed, founder or small team visible.
- **Skip:** brands owned by big groups (e.g. Plenaire → The Hut Group); brands with apps, loyalty programmes or celebrity collabs (signs of an agency or in-house team, e.g. Carrie Elizabeth); brand-new stores with no reviews.

## Changes this needs
- Instagram bio: `Mez Media | Paid ads for UK jewellery & beauty brands`
- Email signature updated to "UK jewellery & beauty brands" (done for new emails).
- Future reels: call to action "Run an online brand?" rather than naming one niche.

## Review
After 2 weeks of sending, compare reply rates by niche and move effort to whichever replies more.
